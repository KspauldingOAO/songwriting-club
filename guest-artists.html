Before you meet the artist

  1. Listen to the selected work sample when it is available.
  2. Choose one lyric, rhythm, melody, or production choice that catches your attention.
  3. Write a question about how the artist made that choice.

Questions from the writing room

  • What came first: the words, melody, rhythm, or central idea?
  • Which part changed the most between the first draft and the finished work?
  • How do you decide whether a line belongs in the song?
  • What does a collaborator help you hear differently?
  • What advice would you give a songwriter finishing their first song?

After the visit: try their craft

Write four to eight new lines using one technique you learned. Explain how the technique affected your choices, and credit the guest who inspired the experiment.
